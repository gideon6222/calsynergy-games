The games

Eight games for Android, each played with one thumb. All free, with ads between rounds and one purchase that removes them.

CandlewrightCandle Maker

Steer candles through wax, light them and sell. A satisfying 3D candle maker.

Steer one batch of candles down a working candle factory and climb from intern to tycoon. Your candles follow the line you steer, so the pools of wax you drive them through decide what comes out the far end. Every five levels the line moves to a new hall, from a daylit workshop to the night shift to a harbor at dawn.

Also listed on Google Play as Candle Gift.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

FangwallDino Evolution Run

Dino run and shoot: collect elements, evolve 16 forms, smash the stone wall.

Start as a raptor and run a road through forests, lava fields, coasts and storm moors. Your thumb steers and aims at once, and the type you shoot most decides what you evolve into. Every world ends at a wall of numbered stone blocks you have to smash your way through.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

NethermereFishing Game

Fish a calm lake at dawn. Go deeper, and the lake grows quietly wrong.

You start on a still lake at dawn in a rowboat, catching small fish close to the surface. Line length decides how deep you can go. Cast, wait, watch the float, then fight each fish on a reel you flick with your thumb.

Also listed on Google Play as Stillwater.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Lawn LegendGrass Cutting

Cozy one-thumb lawn mowing: cut grass, whack weeds, beat bosses, grow the yard.

An overgrown back yard, a stick, and one long summer to take it all back. Mow in rows, whack the weeds, and earn tools you can see in your hand, from a cricket bat to a string trimmer. Buy the gate and the fence moves out into wilder ground.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Load BearingWrecking Ball

Demolition game: swing the wrecking ball, cut columns, escape the slab.

Run a crawler crane on an open street, hold the wrecking ball with your thumb, and bring each building down exactly where you choose. Cut the load-bearing columns and the slab drops. One more column is always worth it, right up until it is not.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Gelbound3D Slime Crate Run

Bounce, spin and smash crates as a living slime. A one-thumb 3D platformer.

You are a drop of living gel tearing through treetops and factories. Tap to jump, swipe to spin, flick down to slam, and break every crate on the way. Bounce crates lead to hidden walkways, secret gems and an extra life.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

DuskrollSnowball Run

Roll a snowball down the mountain, grow it, and build a village from the snow.

Push a snowball off the top of a mountain at dusk and steer it down with one thumb. Everything smaller than the ball sticks to it, and whatever reaches the bottom becomes snow for building the village. A run takes a minute or so.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

MournwellDrill to the Core

Drill down through dead worlds, grab the core, then climb out alive. One thumb.

You are a salvager working a graveyard of dead worlds. Hold to drill down through rock lit only by your own lamp, take the core, then climb back up your tunnel while the world comes apart behind you. Everything you carry makes the climb heavier.

Also listed on Google Play as Gravewell.

Get it on Google Play Privacy

About the studio

Calsynergy Games is one person making small games for phones. Each game starts with one idea you can play with one thumb, then gets tuned until it is worth coming back to.

  • Every game is built and tested on a real phone before it ships.
  • Every game is free to play. Ads show between rounds, never in the middle of one, and one purchase removes them.
  • No accounts, no energy timers, and your progress stays on your phone.
