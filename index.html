About

Calsynergy Games is a one-person studio. Every game starts small on purpose: a single mechanic, tuned until it's worth returning to, shipped as something you can install and play in the gaps of a real day rather than a feed you scroll through.

Everything here is built, tested, and played first on the same phone it's meant for — if a build doesn't hold up on that screen, it doesn't ship.
