About Candlewright

Candlewright is a candle making game for one thumb. In your first minute you drag across a moving conveyor belt, steer a batch of fresh candles through pools of molten wax, and watch them come out the far end dipped, stood up, lit and ready to sell. There is nothing to read first: touch, slide, dip.

Your candles follow the exact line you draw. Line up a pool early and the whole batch goes through it. Swing late and the tail misses. The color of the last pool you cross becomes the color of the whole candle, and every coat you add makes it fatter and worth more. Reach the finish, light every wick and sell the batch to climb from INTERN all the way to TYCOON.

If you like satisfying games, dipping and coating games, ASMR-style factory games or simple one-finger runners, this is a calm, tactile one to pick up for a few minutes or a long evening.

What you do

  • Slide your thumb to steer a whole batch of candles down a candle factory conveyor belt
  • Dip through pools of colored wax to coat every candle in the batch
  • Pick a side where two pools lie next to each other: the last color you cross decides the candle
  • Dodge hazards, spills and crystals that cost you candles
  • Watch the machines press, wrap and tie a bow on your candles
  • Stand your batch on the plinth, light every wick and read your rank

Early levels teach you the belt in a couple of minutes. By the later halls you are reading the pools ahead, choosing colors for an order and deciding which hazard is worth the risk for a dearer candle.

A clean line is the skill

Line up a pool early and the whole batch goes through it. That is a PERFECT. PERFECTs in a row build a streak, and a streak makes the whole batch shine and pay more. Zig-zagging does not pay: holding a clean, steady line does. It is easy to learn and takes a while to do well.

Buy more candles

Between runs, spend what you earned on more candles in the batch, on a guard for your line and on perks. Each extra candle costs more than the last, so every purchase is a small decision.

Every level asks something

Each level comes with an order ticket: so many candles in one color, some with glitter, and a bonus when you deliver. Later levels can lose the whole batch to a crystal hazard, and then the run ends where it stands.

Six factories, and no end

Every few levels the line moves to a new hall, from a daylit workshop to the night shift to a harbor, each with its own twist on the belt and its own music. The street past the finish sells shops, then shop levels, then whole new towns, and it never runs out. Your shops keep selling while you are away, and a small gift waits for you each day.

Keep what you make

  • New candle shapes and skins that unlock as you play
  • A recipe book that records every candle you have ever finished
  • Twelve trophies for the moments that take some doing
  • A daily shift that starts in one tap
